## How the sources fit together

- **Problem:** the notebook diary leads to double bookings (source 3), and missed or cancelled appointments cost salons revenue (sources 4, 5).
- **Existing solutions:** hosted platforms such as Fresha (6) and small PHP systems in academic projects (2, 3).
- **Gap SalonEase fills:** a database where PL/SQL enforces the rules (no double booking, no payment on a cancelled visit, audit log) and a dashboard reads the same data. No Rwandan salon data was found in this search.
- **Future work:** no-show prediction (source 1) using the Kaggle dataset (source 8).
